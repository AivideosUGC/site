FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask

Straight answers — including the limitations.

AI-generated UGC looks "too fake" — will viewers notice?
2026 generation models have improved dramatically on short shots, spoken delivery, and product placement. But let's be honest: in longer videos, eye contact, micro-expressions, and hand movement can still betray the output. Brands should review product appearance, logos, colors, and claims before every launch. AI UGC is strongest for short-hook testing — not for replacing a hero brand film.
What ROAS does AI UGC actually deliver?
It depends on average order value. Below a $25 AOV, AI creative reaches roughly 4.8x ROAS versus 4.5x for human-made. Between $25 and $100 the two are essentially at parity. Above $100, human creative's conversion advantage widens again, because consumer trust in AI-generated content drops by about 14%. AI UGC's clearest edge is in low-AOV, high-frequency DTC testing.
How is AivideosUGC different from Creatify, Arcads, or HeyGen?
We're AivideosUGC, so treat this as a biased comparison — but here's the honest breakdown. Creatify is strong on URL-to-ad and batch testing. Arcads leads on actor realism and conversational delivery. HeyGen is deepest on multilingual localization. Our difference is the full "Product URL → shot-level control → multi-tool matrix" chain. You aren't just generating a talking-head clip — you're completing product-link parsing, scene selection, script refinement, shot assembly, and AI Twin brand cloning inside a single pipeline.
Won't AI generation make our brand visuals inconsistent?
That's the single most common failure point in 2026, clustered around lighting consistency, color accuracy, and motion style. Step 4 of the workflow — "Save & Refine the Storyboard" — exists precisely for this: you audit visual prompts and script lines before render, instead of discovering a shifted product color after generation.
What assets do I need to get started?
The minimum is a single product URL or one high-resolution product image. If you have an authorized likeness of a founder or brand ambassador, AI Twin lets you keep producing script variants indefinitely without ever returning to set.
Does this violate Meta or TikTok ad policy?
In 2026, Meta lists undisclosed AI content as the third-largest category of ad rejections, at roughly 14%. We recommend labeling AI-generated ads and using AI UGC primarily for hook testing and variant iteration, then upgrading validated winners into brand-grade production. Compliance isn't a restriction — it's the precondition for long-term paid media.
